## 3 Conversion

`make -C amiga` writes `amiga/rtl/gen/nanomig.v`: sv2v 0.0.13 `--top=nanomig -DINFER_DPRAM -DENABLE_RTC` over FX68K and the files of `src/nanomig.gprj` below `nanomig` (with `amiga/rtl/src/paula_floppy.v` in place of upstream's), the `` `default_nettype `` directives removed, then `amiga/tools/v2001_fix.py`. `make -C amiga lint`: iverilog 12 and Verilator elaborate it; `amiga/tools/xst_scan.py` reports 0 refusals.

## 5 Defines

| Define | Effect | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `INFER_DPRAM` | Block RAM written as inferable arrays in place of Gowin primitives | Portable to XST |
| `ENABLE_RAM32` | The fast-RAM port addresses 16 MB, so the 8 MB setting is one Zorro II board (without it: 4 MB and boards taken from slow memory) | 8 MB of fast RAM in DDR2 chip A (`amiga_g2.v` FAST RAM) |
| `ENABLE_XSURF` (68020 core only) | The X-Surf 100 board (the edits above) | The network board; the 68000 core is left without it |
| `ENABLE_RTC` | The MSM6242 registers at $DC0000 in `minimig.v` and command 10 in `sysctrl.v` (`companion.v` generated with the same define) | Kickstart's battclock.resource reads the date and time at boot; the firmware sets them from NTP |
| `amiga/rtl/src/cpu_cache_new.v` | Copy of `src/minimig-aga/cpu_cache_new.v` (Rok Krajnc, Alexey Melnikov) with an early hit: in CPU_SM_IDLE, when the cpu did not advance at the previous clock and the previous clock was also CPU_SM_IDLE, a read hit is acknowledged from the tag compare at once (outputs `hit_now`, `hit_dat`), without CPU_SM_READ and CPU_SM_WAIT; such a hit does not rewrite the LRU bit; a read whose RAM outputs are not yet valid is held from `cpu_cs` for one clock (`rd_hold`); inputs `cpu_req`, `cpu_adv_q` | A hit in two core clocks instead of three (sim/cachetest_rom.s: a two-instruction loop 7.9 core clocks an iteration against 11.0 with the upstream state machine and 12.0 without the cache) |
| `amiga/rtl/src/nanomig.v` (cache) | Under `ENABLE_CACHE`: the early hit of `rtl/src/cpu_cache_new.v` in `ram_ready` and `ram_dout`; with CACR bit 0 clear a read is one word on the fast-RAM port (`direct_rd`, behind the write buffer) instead of a line fill; input port `cpu_gap` to `cpu_wrapper` | With the cache off, upstream fills a line for every access (12 core clocks a fetch against 4 without the cache) |
